Vollständige Beschreibung

Asset Management Workload: 80-100% Place of work: Level of experience: Management FlexOffice: on-site Set new standards and shaping the future together. With us, you don't just tick tasks off a list. You work alongside people who take responsibility, set high standards and have the courage to try new approaches. We believe trust and performance go hand in hand. Your ideas, your initiative and your contributions are what really matter to us. You will have the opportunity to make a difference for our customers, your team and your own personal development. Shape the future of Investment Accounting at Group level. In this key leadership role, you will be responsible for Investment Accounting and regulatory affairs for the proprietary assets of our European insurance entities. Together with your team, you will drive the harmonization of processes, systems and data while setting new standards for transparency, governance and efficiency.

What you'll be doing

  • Lead and develop a team of Investment Accounting experts, fostering a culture of trust, collaboration and accountability.
  • Ensure accurate and transparent accounting for our investment portfolio in accordance with IFRS and local accounting standards.
  • Take ownership of regulatory topics relating to SST, Solvency II and tied assets.
  • Advise stakeholders at all levels on complex accounting, regulatory and governance matters.
  • Translate complex financial information into meaningful insights and decision-making support for senior management.
  • Shape and advance our Investment Accounting landscape by driving digitalization, automation, data analytics and AI initiatives.

What you'll bring to the role

  • University degree in Finance, Accounting or Business Administration, complemented by a recognized professional qualification in finance, accounting or auditing.
  • Several years of leadership and project management experience in Investment Accounting, Asset Management, Insurance or Financial Services.
  • Strong knowledge of IFRS 9 and regulatory requirements within the insurance industry.
  • Experience with complex financial instruments as well as accounting or finance transformation initiatives.
  • Strong analytical and communication skills, with the ability to connect people, topics and ideas effectively.
  • Excellent German and English language skills.

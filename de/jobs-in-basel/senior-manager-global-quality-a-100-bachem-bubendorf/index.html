Vollständige Beschreibung

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in Quality, we are looking for a dedicated Senior Manager, Global Quality (a), 100%.

We are looking for an experienced Senior Manager, Global Quality to play a key role in the implementation and successful adoption of Veeva QMS.

In this role, you will work across business functions, Quality, IT, and implementation partners to drive implementation activities from process design and requirements through testing, validation, training, go-live, and operational handover.

This is a hands-on transformation role requiring strong ownership, structured execution, and the ability to collaborate and communicate effectively across functions and organizational levels. You will be expected to proactively identify risks and dependencies, drive resolution of open topics, and ensure assigned activities are delivered to the required quality and timelines.

Your tasks

  • Drive the implementation of Veeva QMS across multiple workstreams, ensuring delivery in line with program priorities, timelines, and quality expectations
  • Contribute to process design, requirements definition, configuration reviews, business alignment, and resolution of design questions, ensuring consistency across processes and workstreams
  • Coordinate cross-functional dependencies, identify risks and gaps, and drive timely resolution of implementation challenges
  • Drive testing, validation, and implementation readiness activities, ensuring all deliverables are complete and inspection-ready
  • Develop and review SOPs, work instructions, training materials, and implementation documentation in alignment with approved processes, system design, and the future operating model
  • Support and guide key users on system and process topics, testing, readiness activities, and knowledge transfer to establish sustainable internal capabilities
  • Coordinate business activities related to data migration, master data, integrations, and system dependencies in close collaboration with IT and key stakeholders
  • Drive stakeholder engagement, change management, communication, training coordination, and user adoption activities
  • Support go-live preparation, hypercare activities, and transition into the future operating model
  • Support operational handover, continuous improvement initiatives, and additional program activities as required

Your profile

  • Completed degree in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Chemistry, Engineering, Quality Management, Information Systems, or a related discipline.
  • Minimum 8 years of relevant experience in pharmaceutical, biotechnology or CDMO
  • Strong hands-on experience with QMS processes, such as deviations, CAPA, change control, document management, training, audits, complaints, and other GxP Quality processes.
  • Strong understanding of Quality Management Systems, GxP processes, CSV, data integrity, and inspection readiness.
  • Proven experience in system implementation or transformation programs, including process design, requirements, testing/UAT, validation, training, and go-live.
  • Hands-on experience with Veeva Vault Quality.
  • English: business fluent
  • German is considered as an advantage
  • Strong ownership, stakeholder management, and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across Quality, business functions, IT, and implementation partners.
  • Structured, pragmatic, and solution-oriented working style with a strong focus on delivery.

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal opportunities with internal development opportunities and continuing education
  • Importance on a healthy work-life balance. That’s why we offer flexible work schedules with the option to work from home and the opportunity to take additional vacation days in exchange for a reduced workload
  • Additional days off (bridge days), which are determined each fall by Bachem
  • On-site daycare center and staff restaurant at the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem, as well as option for extra-mandatory retirement savings through our optional Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms offering discounts from external partners
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
